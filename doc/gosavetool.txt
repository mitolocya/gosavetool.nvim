*gosavetool.txt*  Subtitles, word count and case changes for Neovim

GoSaveTool                                                        *gosavetool*

Convert SRT and WebVTT, shift and repair subtitle timings, count words with
social media and SEO character limits, and change the case of text.
Everything runs inside Neovim, offline.

Made by GoSaveTool, free online tools for creators: https://www.gosavetool.com/

==============================================================================
COMMANDS                                                       *:GoSaveTool*

:[range]GoSaveTool count                               *:GoSaveTool-count*
        Words, characters, sentences, lines and reading time of the visual
        selection, the [range] or the buffer, with the characters left for X
        posts, Instagram and TikTok captions, YouTube titles and descriptions,
        LinkedIn posts, Pinterest descriptions, SEO titles and meta
        descriptions.

:[range]GoSaveTool case {op}                            *:GoSaveTool-case*
        Changes the case of the visual selection (characterwise selections
        too), the [range] or the current line. {op} is one of:
        title     The Lord of the Rings
        sentence  Hello world. How are you?
        upper     UPPERCASE
        lower     lowercase
        slug      olcu-birimi-ceviri
        camel     bestPostingTime
        snake     best_posting_time
        kebab     best-posting-time
        clean     removes trailing spaces, runs of spaces, extra blank lines

:GoSaveTool to_vtt                                     *:GoSaveTool-to_vtt*
:GoSaveTool to_srt                                     *:GoSaveTool-to_srt*
        Opens the subtitles of the buffer converted to WebVTT or SRT in a
        split, in a new buffer named after the file. Save it with |:w|.

:GoSaveTool shift {seconds}                             *:GoSaveTool-shift*
        Moves every cue: 1.5 = later, -2 = earlier.

:GoSaveTool repair                                     *:GoSaveTool-repair*
        Sorts cues, fixes end times before the start and overlapping cues,
        drops empty cues and renumbers.

:GoSaveTool info                                         *:GoSaveTool-info*
        Format, number of cues and the end time.

==============================================================================
LUA                                                          *gosavetool-lua*

require('gosavetool').setup({ keymaps = true })
        Optional. Maps <leader>gc (count), <leader>gt (Title Case),
        <leader>gs (Sentence case), <leader>gu (UPPERCASE) and <leader>gl
        (lowercase) in Normal and Visual mode.

require('gosavetool').statusline()
        "123 words", for a statusline such as lualine.

require('gosavetool.subtitles') and require('gosavetool.text') are the
libraries: parse(), convert(), shift(), repair(), stats(), limits(), title()
and the rest, as in the @gosavetool/subtitles and @gosavetool/text packages.
